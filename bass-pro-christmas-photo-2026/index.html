Weekdays Are Your Friend

Weekends are packed—and not just a little. Long lines, cranky kids, and rushed photos. If you can swing it, aim for a weekday afternoon. The crowd thins out, and you’ll feel like you have the whole set to yourself.

Early or Late? Pros and Cons

Early mornings often mean fresher setups and less foot traffic. But sometimes, the lighting can be harsh or cold (literally). Late afternoons offer softer natural light, but you risk hitting peak hours.

In real-world testing at last year’s event, I found that arriving right at opening on a Tuesday gave me the sweet spot: a calm atmosphere and just enough daylight for crisp photos.
