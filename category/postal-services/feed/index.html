Can I use Christmas stamps on packages or only letters?

Christmas stamps can be used on any mail item that requires First-Class postage, including letters, cards, and packages within the appropriate weight limits.

Yes. The 2026 release includes limited edition press sheets with foil accents and artist signature replicas, making them highly collectible.

How can I keep my Christmas stamps in good condition for collecting?

Store them in protective sleeves away from direct sunlight and moisture. Avoid bending or folding the sheets to preserve their condition and value.
