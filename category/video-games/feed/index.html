Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Fortnite Christmas 2026 event start and end?

The event kicks off on December 10th, 2026, and runs through January 3rd, 2027, giving players nearly a month to dive into holiday madness.

Are the Christmas event rewards permanent?

Yes, all skins, emotes, and weapon wraps earned during the event stay in your locker permanently once unlocked.

Can I play the Christmas challenges solo or only in squads?

Challenges are designed for all modes — solo, duo, or squads — so you can go it alone or team up for extra fun.

Does the event affect competitive play?

The holiday hazards are disabled in official competitive modes, but casual and public matches get the full festive experience.

What’s the best strategy for the Frostbite Zone?

Keep moving and plan routes to warming stations. Carrying hot cocoa items boosts survival, so prioritize those pickups early.

In my experience, the Frostbite Zone turned what would usually be a camping strategy into a sprint contest. It’s chaotic, but that’s what makes it thrilling.
