When does the OSRS Christmas Event 2026 start and end?

The event kicks off on December 15, 2026, and runs through January 5, 2027. You’ve got just over three weeks to dive in and claim your rewards.

Yes, but certain quests and minigames require a minimum combat level of 30 and completion of basic holiday tutorials. Don’t worry; the event’s friendly to newcomers.

Most gear and cosmetics you earn are permanent, but some event-exclusive items in the shop disappear after the event closes. So grab what you want before January 5.
