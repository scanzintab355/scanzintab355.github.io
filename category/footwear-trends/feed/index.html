How to Score Christmas Jordans 2026: Insider Tips and Tricks

Trying to cop Christmas Jordans feels like running a gauntlet—online bots, midnight launches, and stores selling out in seconds. But don’t sweat it; here’s how to get ahead.

  1. Sign up early: Nike’s SNKRS app and boutique sites often give early access to loyal customers. Create profiles now, fill out your info, and set reminders.
  2. Use multiple devices: I’ve found that entering raffles on both phone and laptop simultaneously ups your chances. Don’t overdo it, though—too many entries can backfire.
  3. Local shops matter: Some smaller sneaker stores get allocations that don’t hit big online drops. Visiting them can feel like hunting for treasure, but it’s worth the gamble.
  4. Stay social: Follow sneaker forums and insider accounts. Sometimes, surprise restocks or additional releases pop up last minute.
