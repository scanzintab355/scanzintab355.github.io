Frequently Asked Questions

Are boxed Christmas cards more expensive than single cards?

Yes, boxed cards usually cost more upfront because you’re buying in bulk, but the per-card price is often lower. Plus, you get consistent design and quality, making them worth the investment.

Can I customize boxed Christmas cards in 2026?

Absolutely. Many brands now offer online customization options—adding names, photos, or even custom messages. It’s a popular feature that makes the cards feel less “mass-produced.”

What’s the best time to buy boxed Christmas cards in 2026?

Early November is prime. You snag the best selections before stocks run low, plus you avoid last-minute shipping headaches. Some retailers even drop early-bird discounts.

Do eco-friendly boxed cards look as good as traditional ones?

They’re not only comparable—they often look better. Sustainable card makers have upped their design game, proving going green doesn’t mean dull or cheap.
