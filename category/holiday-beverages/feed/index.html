Frequently Asked Questions

When do Starbucks Christmas drinks launch in 2026?

Typically, the holiday menu drops in early November. This gives you plenty of time to savor the festive lineup before Christmas.

Are there vegan-friendly options in the 2026 Christmas lineup?

Yes! Starbucks offers plant-based milks like oat, almond, and soy for most holiday drinks. Just specify your preference when ordering.

Can I customize the sweetness level in Christmas drinks?

Absolutely. You can ask for fewer pumps of syrup or request sugar-free versions of certain flavors, depending on the drink.

Are these drinks available internationally?

Most Christmas drinks appear in Starbucks locations worldwide, but some flavors might be region-specific based on local tastes.
