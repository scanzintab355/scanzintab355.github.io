Frequently Asked Questions

Are supermarkets closed on Christmas Day 2026?

Most major supermarkets are closed on December 25, 2026. Some may open limited hours on Christmas Eve.

Is public transportation available on Christmas?

Public transit typically runs on a reduced or holiday schedule, so expect fewer buses and trains.

Can I find restaurants open on Christmas?

Yes, but they’re limited. Upscale hotels and select chains often offer special menus—reservations are essential.

Do closures apply to online shopping?

Online stores may still accept orders, but shipping and customer service might be delayed due to holiday closures.
