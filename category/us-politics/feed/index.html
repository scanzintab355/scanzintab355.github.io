Understanding Federal Holidays: How Does a Day Become Official?

Federal holidays aren’t just tossed around by presidential tweets or quick announcements. They require a formal process—Congress passes a law, and the president signs it. Simple as that.

What’s the Process?

First, a bill must be introduced in Congress to designate a new federal holiday. Then, it goes through committees, debates, votes, and if approved, it lands on the president’s desk.

Without that stamp of approval, no matter who’s president, the day doesn’t officially become a federal holiday.
