That means federal agencies aren’t required by law to close on Christmas Eve. Instead, it’s up to each agency’s leadership or the President’s executive decisions to make that call.

What Would It Take for Christmas Eve to Become a Federal Holiday?

Simply put: Congress would have to pass a law making Christmas Eve an official federal holiday. The President would then need to sign it.

Legislative Hurdles

In 2026, Christmas Eve hasn’t made the legislative docket. Lawmakers prioritize economic policies and infrastructure, leaving holiday changes on the back burner.

Plus, adding a federal holiday impacts the economy—government offices close, productivity drops, and costs rise. That tends to make Congress hesitant.

The Trump Factor

Trump’s influence is mostly in the political arena, not the day-to-day holiday schedule. Even if Trump expressed personal interest, he can’t unilaterally declare holidays without Congress.
