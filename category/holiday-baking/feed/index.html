Use Fresh Ingredients—No Exceptions

Expired baking powder or old nuts? Toss them. Freshness in 2026 baking is non-negotiable. I once tried baking cookies with stale baking soda—disaster. The cookies didn’t rise and tasted flat.

Chill That Dough

Chilling dough for at least an hour is a 2026 must-do. It prevents spreading and boosts flavor. One friend swore by overnight chilling, which gave her cookies a perfect snap and a richer taste.

Don’t Skip the Cooling Rack

Let cookies cool on wire racks. Trying to plate hot cookies is like juggling hot potatoes—they fall apart or steam themselves soggy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the easiest cookie for beginners in 2026?

Start with classic sugar cookies but add a twist like cardamom or lemon zest to keep it fresh and festive.

Are there healthy Christmas cookie options this year?

Yes! Many recipes now incorporate alternative flours, less sugar, and nuts or oats for fiber. Gluten-free and vegan options are also widely available.

How can I prevent my cookies from spreading too much?

Chill your dough before baking and avoid using too much butter or oil. Also, baking on parchment paper helps keep shape.

Should I decorate cookies before or after baking?

Most decorations go on after baking once the cookies are cool, unless the recipe specifies otherwise (like with some glazes).
